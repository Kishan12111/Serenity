# Serinity - Anime-Inspired Focus & Study Companion

**The world's first free Pomodoro timer with comprehensive focus statistics and anime-inspired ambient themes.**

Transform your study sessions with beautiful aesthetics. Track your focus time, build streaks, and boost productivity—completely free, forever.

## ✨ Why Serinity?

- **100% Free** - All features including cloud sync and statistics are completely free
- **Beautiful Aesthetics** - Anime-inspired wallpapers create a calm, immersive environment
- **Comprehensive Stats** - Track daily, weekly, monthly progress with beautiful charts
- **Cloud Sync** - Create a free account to sync across all your devices
- **Proven Technique** - Built around the Pomodoro Technique for maximum productivity

## Features

### 🎯 Focus Timer
- **Custom Timer**: Set any duration from 1 to 120 minutes
- **Pomodoro Mode**: Pre-configured focus sessions (25 min) with customizable breaks
- **Circular Progress Indicator**: Visual feedback with breathing animation while focusing
- **Keyboard Support**: Press spacebar to start/pause anytime
- **Session Tracking**: Automatically logs completed focus sessions

### 🔐 User Authentication
- **Free Account Creation** - Sign up to save your progress
- **Anonymous Mode** - Use without creating an account
- **Data Migration** - Anonymous data transfers when you create an account
- **Secure Storage** - All data stored securely in Convex database
- **Cross-Device Sync** - Access your stats from any device

## How to Use

1. **Focus Page**: Set your timer and start a focus session
   - Choose custom minutes or switch to Pomodoro mode
   - Press spacebar or click Start to begin
   - Watch the circular progress indicator fill
   - Get notified when your session completes

2. **Stats Page**: Monitor your progress
   - See daily, weekly, and monthly focus time
   - Track your current streak
   - Analyze trends with charts

3. **Settings Page**: Personalize your experience
   - Pick your favorite wallpaper
   - Toggle animations on/off
   - Adjust Pomodoro durations
   - Change ambient sound preference

4. **Account**: Sync your progress
   - Click the user icon in the navigation
   - Sign up for a free account
   - Your data syncs automatically across devices

## Data Storage

### With Account (Recommended)
- All data stored securely in Convex database
- Syncs across all your devices
- Never lose your progress
- Includes: statistics, streaks, settings, session history

### Without Account
- Data stored locally in browser (localStorage)
- Works offline
- Can migrate to account anytime

## Technical Stack

- **Framework**: Next.js 16 with App Router
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4 with custom animations
- **Charts**: Recharts for data visualization
- **UI Components**: Shadcn/ui
- **Icons**: Lucide React
- **Animations**: Custom CSS keyframes with smooth transitions

## Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Spacebar**: Start/Pause timer

## Tips for Best Results

- Start with 25-minute focus sessions followed by 5-minute breaks
- Take a longer 15-minute break after 4 consecutive focus sessions
- Choose a wallpaper that matches your mood and energy level
- Keep your daily streak alive for motivation
- Take regular breaks and stay hydrated
- Disable notifications if you want complete silence
